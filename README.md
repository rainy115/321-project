# CLASSINATOR
## Our Chosen System
### Assignment Tracker
This semester our team will build a Scheduling Assistant composed of an Assignment Manager and Course Planner. web application that converts .ics into parsable data that can be uploaded in MongoDB paired with a course planner to help students choose their course schedules. It will be called Classinator.

Canvas has the feature to let students export their assignments and due dates as a calendar feed (an .ics file). It will then import that feed into a database, then display it as a calendar interface organized by time of day instead of day by day list. Students can also add their own events and plans manually so the schedule reflects their real life, not just coursework. 

Our product will integrate an AI assistant derived from PatriotAI called Claudia. This AI assistant will act like a personal advisor. Claudia reads a student’s imported courses and self-added events and builds a prioritized list of what to tackle each day. A sidebar shows the day’s deadlines, similar to the Canvas homepage but ranked by Claudia rather than the listed in due date order. Our core scope for the semester is the Canvas import, the database, the calendar interface, the sidebar, and a working version of Claudia’s prioritization.

### Course Planner
The second part of the system, the Course Planner is an AI-assisted academic planning system designed to help students determine which courses they should take and when they should take them in order to efficiently complete their degree requirements. Unlike traditional degree-planning tools such as Degree Works, which primarily allow students to track and manually arrange courses, the Course Planner actively recommends courses based on a student’s degree requirements, completed coursework, prerequisites, interests, and preferred schedule.

The system will provide students with information about available Mason courses, including course descriptions, credits, prerequisites, and scheduling information. An AI-powered recommendation system will suggest courses that best fit the student’s academic goals and interests, while ranking logic will prioritize options based on factors such as degree requirements, prerequisite availability, course relevance, and the student’s planned graduation timeline.

Students will be able to organize recommended courses into semester-by-semester plans and visualize how their degree path may progress. Once a semester plan is finalized, students can import their selected courses into an integrated calendar and assignment tracker, automatically incorporating course meeting times and creating a day-by-day academic schedule from the selected classes.

The goal of the Course Planner is to provide a more proactive and personalized alternative to existing degree-planning tools. Rather than simply showing students what requirements remain, the system helps answer the more practical question: “What courses should I take next, and how will they fit into my semester?” By combining degree planning, AI-powered course recommendations, course information, and calendar scheduling into one system, the Course Planner helps students make more informed decisions about their academic path while reducing the effort required to plan each semester.
